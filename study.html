Design Study

How the notation was chosen — the goals, the candidate grammars, the measurements on real production data, and the trade-offs. ← back to the workspace

Goals and constraints

  • Generated automatically from a product's real stone composition;
  • As short as possible;
  • Readable;
  • Detailed only when necessary — the common case should not pay for the rare one;
  • Constraint: avoid collisions — a collision is two different entities sharing the same code.

Every measurement below comes from the real production history: 247,898 stone-composition lines, 45,895 products with a composition, and dictionaries of 112 stone types, 115 shades and 178 shapes.

Residual, whatever the grammar: 12.3 % of products (5,661) are true twins — identical stones down to shade and shape, differing only by count, size or layout, which no colour code encodes. An optional bracket suffix ([…]) is reserved for them; product-code uniqueness is enforced by the software either way.

The Emasur proposal, in detail

Emasur's validated lists propose 201 flat stone identities where the colour is fused into the code with a dot (AG Agate, W.AG White Agate, BK.AG Black Agate, PK.AG Pink Agate…), plus separate lists of 13 shapes and 13 lapidary cuts.

KeptWhy / why not
Commercial identities carry their colour ✔ kept it is how the trade names articles — in the retained design a colour-bearing stone declares an implied hue, and writing another hue on it is refused as a double colour
Readable mnemonic codes ✔ kept the dictionaries use the same spirit (SA, DI, 1P Pink…)
Variable-width codes with dots ✘ not kept tokens cannot be concatenated unambiguously; average 5.39 characters for the identity alone, up to 10 (PTO.FANCY)
No quality axis ✘ not kept the history grades stones massively (A/AA/AAA depth on 38.7 % of lines, Good/Medium/Bad on precious stones): without a grade block, all of them collide
No product-code rule ✘ completed the goal is a code generated from the composition: the retained design adds the deterministic ordering (center stone first, then heaviest descending)

Why the retained design answers each goal

GoalDesign feature
Generated automatically the product code is derived from the real composition with a deterministic order — center first, heaviest next, ties by token
As short as possible 3.20 characters on average; the shortest grammar that loses no information without exploding the dictionary
Readable mnemonic dictionary codes, and separator-free parsing: a lone digit is a grade, digit+letter is a hue, letters are a shape — at most one reading of any token is structurally valid
Detailed only when necessary defaults per stone, determined from occurrence counts in the history, are omitted — the frequent case writes in 2 characters
No collisions the full stone triplet is encoded (0 product collisions measured); double colours are structurally refused; the 12.3 % true twins keep the reserved […] suffix
The retained grammar is the user-proposed fixed-width design with the one-digit grade refinement — Emasur's commercial colour-bearing identities are honoured through implied hues, its variable-width dotted codes and missing axes are not.
